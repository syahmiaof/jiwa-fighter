## 4. Visual Identity & Design Consistency Notes

1. **Color Harmony:**
   - Obsidian Black (`#0C0D0F`) forms the deep, cinematic base for dark sections, navigation, and badges.
   - Antique Brass (`#C6A260`) serves as the signature metallic accent for active navigation, badges, borders, and buttons.
   - Bone White / Warm Stone (`#F0ECE3`) provides the editorial contrast for alternating light sections and readable body typography.
   - Deep Oxblood / Crimson (`#621F24`) adds martial energy in the Silat Series and campaign textures.
2. **Typography System:**
   - Display Headlines: Ultra-bold, condensed, uppercase sans-serif (`font-display`) with tight tracking and powerful punch.
   - Subheadings & Kicker Labels: Spaced uppercase sans-serif with subtle gold accent lines.
   - Body & Meta: Clean, legible neutral sans-serif with balanced line-height.
3. **Motif & Texture Language:**
   - Malay songket and wood-carving geometric chevrons and diamonds are subtly woven into diagonals, borders, and background watermarks.
   - Clean, functional grids with generous negative space prevent visual clutter.
